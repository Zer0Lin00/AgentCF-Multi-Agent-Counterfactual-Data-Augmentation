## 一、引入的数理工程知识梳理

### 1.1 核心数学方法

#### **Wasserstein距离（Optimal Transport Theory）**
- **位置**: `src/metrics/minimality.py:24-36`
- **理论基础**: Wasserstein-1距离（Earth Mover's Distance）
- **应用场景**: 衡量原文与反事实文本的词嵌入分布差异
- **实现细节**:
  - 将词嵌入投影到主成分方向（PCA-like降维）
  - 计算1维Wasserstein距离: `wasserstein_distance(proj_o, proj_c)`
  - 归一化映射: `score = max(0, 1 - dist/2)`，使得距离越小分数越高
- **优势**: 相比简单的编辑距离，Wasserstein距离能捕捉语义空间中的细微变化，对同义替换更敏感

#### **Domain Invariance Score（域不变性评分）**
- **位置**: `src/agents/verifier.py:83-89`
- **理论基础**: Domain Adaptation理论中的域不变特征学习
- **计算方式**: `domain_invariant = min(label_score, general_score)`
- **设计思想**: 
  - `label_score`: SST-2特定模型的预测置信度
  - `general_score`: 通用情感模型的预测置信度
  - 取最小值确保样本在两个域上都具有鲁棒性
- **理论意义**: 强制生成的反事实样本不依赖于特定域的spurious features

#### **Style Robustness Score（风格鲁棒性评分）**
- **位置**: `src/agents/verifier.py:163-175` (旧版), `src/agents/verifier.py:229-238` (新版)
- **设计原理**: 基于词汇捷径检测（Lexical Shortcut Detection）
- **惩罚机制**:
  - **Lexical Shortcut Penalty**: 仅改变2个以内情感词且总改动≤4词 → 惩罚0.25-0.30
  - **Template Penalty**: 单一情感词替换 → 惩罚0.15
  - **Short Penalty**: 文本长度<8词 → 惩罚0.20
  - **Context Bonus**: 包含推理连接词（because, although等）→ 奖励0.12-0.14
- **理论依据**: McCoy et al. (2019) 关于NLU模型对表面线索的过度依赖

#### **Consistency Score（一致性评分）**
- **位置**: `src/agents/verifier.py:137-160` (旧版), `src/agents/verifier.py:203-226` (新版)
- **改进**: 从字符串匹配升级为语义相似度
- **实现**: 检查Planner指定的`elements_to_preserve`是否在原文和候选文本中都保留
- **理论意义**: 确保反事实干预的可控性（Counterfactual Intervention Controllability）

#### **Difficulty Score（样本难度评分）**
- **位置**: `PROGRESS.md:11-14`提到但未在当前代码中激活
- **理论基础**: Curriculum Learning和Active Learning
- **计算方式**: 分类器置信度越接近0.5，难度分数越高
- **潜在价值**: 优先选择边界样本，增强模型对决策边界的学习

### 1.2 多智能体协作框架

#### **分层规划与执行（Hierarchical Planning）**
- **Planner Agent**: 显式区分因果特征（causal features）与伪相关特征（spurious features）
- **Generator Agent**: 基于规划执行最小干预
- **Verifier Agent**: 多维度质量评估（7个评分维度）
- **Selector Agent**: 动态阈值筛选（Dynamic Percentile Filtering）

#### **闭环反馈机制（Closed-Loop Refinement）**
- **最大重试轮数**: 2轮
- **反馈类型**: 可执行的失败原因（actionable critique）
- **理论基础**: Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)的简化版

### 1.3 评分融合策略

#### **加权线性组合（Weighted Linear Combination）**
当前权重配置（`src/agents/verifier.py:180-188`）:
```
label_score:              0.25
general_sentiment_score:  0.10
domain_invariant_score:   0.18
semantic_score:           0.18
consistency_score:        0.14
style_robustness_score:   0.10
minimality_score:         0.05
```

**设计理念**: 
- 标签正确性（0.25）和域不变性（0.18）权重最高 → 确保基本质量
- 语义保持（0.18）和一致性（0.14）次之 → 保证可控性
- 风格鲁棒性（0.10）和最小性（0.05）权重较低 → 避免过度保守

### 2.2 **方法部分增强**

#### 新增子章节: "3.5 Domain-Invariant Quality Assurance"

**建议内容**:
```markdown
To mitigate domain-specific spurious correlations, we introduce three complementary mechanisms:

**Domain Invariance Score**: We evaluate each counterfactual using both a task-specific classifier (DistilBERT fine-tuned on SST-2) and a general sentiment model. The domain invariance score is defined as:
$$
s_{\text{domain}} = \min(s_{\text{task}}, s_{\text{general}})
$$
This ensures the generated sample exhibits robust sentiment signals across different model priors.

**Style Robustness Score**: Inspired by recent work on lexical shortcuts [McCoy et al., 2019], we penalize samples that rely on minimal lexical changes:
$$
s_{\text{style}} = 0.78 \cdot r_{\text{length}} + 0.12 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{\text{context}} - \lambda_{\text{shortcut}}
$$
where $r_{\text{length}}$ is the length ratio, $\mathbb{1}_{\text{context}}$ indicates presence of reasoning markers (e.g., "because", "although"), and $\lambda_{\text{shortcut}}$ penalizes templates with ≤2 sentiment word changes.

**Wasserstein-based Minimality**: Beyond edit distance, we optionally employ Wasserstein-1 distance on word embedding distributions to capture semantic-level minimality:
$$
d_W = W_1(\mu_{\text{orig}}, \mu_{\text{cf}})
$$
where $\mu$ represents the empirical distribution of word embeddings projected onto the principal semantic axis.
```

### 2.4 **新增讨论章节: "6.3 The Quality-Robustness Paradox"**

```markdown
Our results reveal an intriguing paradox: AgentCF generates counterfactuals with higher semantic similarity (61.47% vs. 60.72%) and edit similarity (57.50% vs. 56.45%) compared to Single-LLM baselines, yet exhibits a larger OOD robustness gap.

We hypothesize two explanations:

1. **Over-fitting to Task-Specific Features**: The multi-agent framework's explicit planning may inadvertently encode SST-2-specific patterns (e.g., movie review structures) that do not transfer to IMDb's longer, more diverse reviews.

2. **Quality Metrics Misalignment**: Current automatic metrics (label success, semantic similarity) may not capture the true desiderata for OOD robustness. The style robustness score (Section 3.5) was designed to address this, but its weight (0.10) may be insufficient.

**Future Work**: Incorporating domain-adversarial training objectives or meta-learning across multiple source domains could help AgentCF balance in-domain performance with cross-domain generalization.
```
